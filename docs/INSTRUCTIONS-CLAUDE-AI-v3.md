## 5. SNOMED CT governance — hard rules

1. **Sole verification authority: CSIRO Ontoserver, CT-AU edition** (`r4.ontoserver.csiro.au/fhir`). The International browser (browser.ihtsdotools.org) is **banned** as a verification source — it caused the documented 7–40% accuracy failures.
2. **Any LLM-generated code is unverified until Ontoserver-confirmed.** No code ships with `// VERIFY` remaining.
3. **Banned UK Extension ranges** (NOT_FOUND in CT-AU): `234xxx`, `172xxx`, `122xxx`, `14xxx`, `265xxx`, `274xxx`, `284xxx`.
4. AU-only codes must carry a documented International-edition fallback for multi-jurisdiction export.
5. Mechanics: `CodeSystem/$lookup` → `OperationOutcome` = NOT_FOUND; `parameter[name=display]` = preferred term. ECL `<< 71388002` for procedures, `<< 404684003` for diagnoses. Batch ≤10–12 codes per JavaScript execution (Claude in Chrome against a live Ontoserver tab) to avoid truncation.
6. Display names in picklists match official preferred terms unless a deliberate interface-layer divergence is documented.

## 6. Product architecture principles

- **Assessment-first:** the specialty assessment module captures clinical detail; diagnosis and procedure suggestions are *outputs* of assessment, never inputs. HandTraumaAssessment is the gold-standard pattern.
- **60-second rule:** an uncomplicated case must be loggable in under 60 seconds (~12 taps). Every feature is evaluated against theatre-floor reality.
- **Zero-tap happy path:** the system infers, the surgeon confirms.
- **Progressive disclosure, inline over modal** for primary operative data; hub-and-spoke modal sheets for specialty detail modules; module visibility is data-driven.
- **Structured data only** for anything queryable/exportable. Free text is the enemy of audit, research, and registry export.
- Registry export is a presentation-layer problem: capture the superset of registry fields once; mapping happens at export.

## 7. Team sharing & EPA design principles

- **Bidirectional double-blind:** supervisor rates trainee top-down (operative performance); trainee rates supervisor bottom-up (teaching quality); simultaneous reveal.
- **Operative role and supervision hierarchy are fully independent dimensions.** A consultant scrubbing as First Assistant is still the supervisor.
- EPA assessments follow **adjacent links in the seniority chain only** (each pair = one teacher + one learner), mapped through the universal 6-tier career-stage model (RACS / ISCP–JCST / Weiterbildungsordnung / SIWF-FMH / Poland / ACGME).
- **The procedure IS the activity:** SNOMED CT code is the EPA identifier; no separate EPA catalog.
- **E2EE zero-knowledge relay:** server-relayed but server-blind for patient data; three-blob case structure. On-device patient identity (name, DOB, NHI) is never transmitted; NHI is per-user HMAC-SHA256 hashed.
- Shared cases include full patient identity because all tagged members were part of the care team — the privacy boundary is the server, not the team.
- Academic framing: the "scarce resource" thesis (1–2 trainees learn from an operation vs ~30 from a ward round) is the central argument of the EPA papers. SNOMED CT is presented as pragmatic engineering, not educational innovation.

## 8. Regulatory standing

- Opus is a **documentation tool, not clinical decision support**. Procedure suggestions are logging convenience. Margin recommendations cross into clinical guidance and **must not be displayed**.
- Jurisdictional frame: NZ Privacy Act 2020 + Health Information Privacy Code 2020; GDPR for EU markets; EPA assessments are personal data about identifiable colleagues.
- German **§203 StGB** and Swiss **Art. 321 StGB** create criminal liability for team sharing without patient consent — a consent system is a legal prerequisite before team sharing ships in DE/CH.
- Patent: rejected. Trademark: "Opus Logbook" registered (NZ).
